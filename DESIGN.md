# Ceramic desktop reading

## Direction
Ivory-inspired compact reading workspace, built only with official MyGo v0.7.4. A narrow navigation rail and independent per-tab reading paths replace the earlier broad sidebar. Real content, not decoration, provides the visual focus.

## Palette
System light/dark background, surface, text, muted text and borders. Accent is monochrome ink, matching the black-and-white app icon: #1d1d1f (hover #3a3a3c, pressed #000000, white text) in light mode; #ededed (hover #ffffff, pressed #d4d4d6, #1d1d1f text) in dark mode. Always set hover and pressed with the accent so framework defaults never leak through. The unread badge is the only warm color: clay #b34a30 with white text in light mode, #e2876b with #1d1d1f text in dark mode. Selection uses the text color at 9% opacity with an accent glyph. No forced dark mode or custom glass. Post-type gradients remain subtle and are omitted in high contrast.

## Typography
MyGo system typography. Post body 14 pt, line height 1.55; author and column title 14 pt semibold; account 12 pt; time/metadata 11 pt. Profile title 23 pt. Selectable body and biographies retain links, emphasis and paragraphs.

## Components
Official Toolbar, SearchField, Segmented, List, Avatar, RichText, ButtonBase, MenuButton and Icon. Navigation icons retain accessible labels and shortcut tooltips. Images use 10 pt corners; link previews 7 pt; selected navigation 10 pt. Avatars are circular. Reply opens the composer with the original post as its reply target; boost, favourite and bookmark controls toggle their state after server confirmation, with nonzero counts and accessible labels. Loaded images open in-app without a redundant external link beneath them.

## Layout
88 pt minimum navigation rail, expanded if native controls need more room. On macOS, the hidden title bar keeps real traffic lights at (12,18), with a 52 pt reserved draggable area. Windows and Linux keep the system title bar, with no reserved area and no vibrancy. The account avatar or signed-out profile icon sits above Home and Search. Reading columns have 12 pt outer padding, 12 pt gaps and 12 pt rounded corners. Each tab starts with one left-aligned root column, up to 580 pt wide. Details append to the right in a horizontal scroll container that reveals the latest column. When at least 720 pt is available, two columns fit side by side; wider windows can show more. Every column has a 48 pt toolbar and independent virtual list. Posts use 14 pt vertical / 16 pt horizontal padding, 42 pt avatars, a 12 pt avatar/body gap and 6 pt content spacing. Footer actions group along the text edge with 30 pt hit height; bookmark and external link sit at the trailing edge.

When at least 260 pt of workspace is left right of the reading columns, the Ceramic figure from the app icon, mirrored to face the columns, peeks up from the bottom edge, centered three quarters of the way across that space: 168 pt wide, with 22% of its cropped body below the edge. It is ink #1c1c1e with white eyes, or #38383c with #eeeef0 eyes in dark mode. Only the eyes move: they spring toward the pointer while it is over the workspace, up to 55% of their radius, and blink every 4.6 s, twice every third time. It never takes input or focus. When columns fill the space it springs below the edge; Reduce Motion keeps it still and moves it without animation.

## Depth
White reading panels (#ffffff) float on a shared light gray workspace (#eeeef0), with a quiet 2 pt offset / 8 pt blur / 4% black shadow. Dark mode uses pure black (#000000) for the workspace, login and dialog backgrounds, near-black reading panels (#0a0a0a), and slightly lighter headers (#111111). Each panel clips its contents to rounded corners. Both appearances use official MyGo VibrancySidebar (macOS NSVisualEffectView sidebar material) behind a transparent rail. Reading columns paint an opaque background. When vibrancy is unavailable the rail paints the system surface in light mode and black in dark mode. Controls and link previews use the system surface. Reading columns keep their permanent shadow; individual posts receive only a soft temporary hover shadow. Images open in a window-wide modal overlay above the unchanged reading columns. The backdrop is transparent, without dimming; only the image, compact controls and caption have visible surfaces. Left/right arrows navigate images from the originating post, excluding video, without wrapping. Escape, the close button or a click outside the viewer dismisses it without activating underlying content or moving the reading position.

## Interaction rules
Home, Search and Profile each retain their own column path and horizontal offset. Authors and conversations append within the originating tab. Closing a focused detail removes only that column and focuses its left neighbor. Closing an unfocused detail preserves the current focus. New searches reset only the Search path. Lists retain reading anchors; background responses belong to their originating feed, and logout invalidates them. No automatic profile prefetch or decorative idle animation; animated GIF avatars play only while visible and stop for reduced motion. Held reading keys request animation frames only while scrolling.

## Window sizes
Below 720 pt of content width, show only the focused column of the active tab. Default window 1240x820; minimum 420x560. Check both sides of the breakpoint, minimum height and dark/light appearances. The same state objects survive resizing.

## Future changes
Keep reading columns independently scrollable and all controls keyboard accessible. Use the existing semantic theme roles and compact type scale. Extend content types through the shared post renderer. Do not add framework patches, a local MyGo replacement, simulated AppKit controls, or write actions under a read-only authorization.

Each tab remembers a focused column. Pointer presses inside it select it without consuming child actions. In multi-column paths, focus uses a 1 pt accent border at 32% opacity in light mode and #666666 in dark mode and 6 pt offset / 16 pt blur black shadow, at 16% opacity in light mode and 40% in dark mode, with no layout movement. Single-column paths keep the quiet base outline; native keyboard focus stays enabled. Cmd-W closes only a focused detail; on the root it closes the window.

Post types use subtle fading tints: boosts #9263c7, replies #4f89b7, warnings/sensitive #c48a38, 6.5% opacity in light and 10% in dark; ordinary posts remain untinted. High contrast omits decorative gradients. Folded warnings and sensitive media render abstract shapes with Gaussian sigma 5, not actual text or images; reveal is explicit and reversible. Server hide filters exclude posts, including nested reblogs.

Only the sidebar uses system vibrancy, in both light and dark modes. Reading columns use the framework's continuous rounded corners with opaque light headers (#f7f7f9), white bodies and a gray workspace (#eeeef0). Dark mode uses #111111 headers, #0a0a0a bodies and a #000000 workspace. Column outlines use #333333, rising to #666666 for the focused column in multi-column paths; high contrast retains the system border. Separators and hover fills retain hierarchy. No custom corner masks or transparent header cutouts.

Content lists share one hover surface across posts, notifications and accounts (including search, following and followers). Rows have no divider lines; spacing separates entries. The inner surface lifts by 1 pt over 140 ms with a soft shadow and quiet tint, while its hit area and virtual-list geometry stay fixed. Text selection freezes movement. Reduced motion disables displacement, and high contrast omits shadows. Native autocomplete and menu rows retain their framework feedback.

External link previews use a compact horizontal layout: a fixed 64 × 64 pt square thumbnail beside provider, a two-line title and a two-line summary. Posts’ attached photos retain their full media layout.

Post and notification text opens a conversation immediately on a single click. Drag selection, copying and clearing a selection do not navigate. Reply opens the composer immediately; view-post controls open the conversation immediately.

A conversation opens only once per workspace. Reopening it reveals the existing column without resetting its reading position.

Image cache limits apply to unused history; images used by the current view stay resident to prevent eviction/reload flicker. After display, decoded bitmaps release redundant CPU pixels while retaining GPU textures and compressed data for restoration.

Post visibility uses a small icon and text label alongside the account handle: public, quiet public, followers, or private mention. Restricted audiences use the theme accent without adding a card border or motion. Tooltips explain the audience; unknown or missing visibility is never presented as public. Boosts show the original post’s visibility, and notification excerpts retain the same labels.

Post rows have fixed outer hit areas with 4 pt vertical and 8 pt horizontal spacing, no divider lines, and an 8 pt corner radius. Pointer hover lifts the inner surface by 1 pt over 140 ms with a soft shadow. Text selection freezes the current displacement until release; ordinary controls settle on press. Reduced motion disables displacement, and high contrast omits shadow. Existing content-type tints remain.

Precise touchpad input locks to one axis and one vertical reading target per gesture, including momentum. Small opening deltas accumulate to 3 pt and require a 1.6:1 direction advantage; ambiguous starts wait up to 12 pt. The initial direction can be corrected within 80 ms until 12 pt of net displacement, without rewinding either view; edges never transfer input to the other axis. MyGo v0.7.4 exposes no native gesture phases, so a 180 ms idle gap or fingers interrupting momentum starts a new gesture. Mouse wheels retain framework behavior.

The signed-out login page uses a centered, borderless form up to 360 pt wide, the bundled Ceramic app icon, a single instance field and a compact Continue action. Field labels, instance input text, helper text, authorization status and Cancel all align centrally. The 24 pt title and 72 pt logo stay centered. A full-width Continue action gives way to an inline loading status during authorization, with the browser instruction and Cancel below it; permission guidance appears only before authorization. Keep explanatory text brief; retain the navigation rail and native window drag area. Narrow and short windows scroll the form without clipping controls.

The instance field suggests up to six domains from the bundled directory as the user types: domains starting with the text first, with mastodon.social leading its prefix, then domains with a later label starting with it, then any containing it. An exact match shows no popup. Suggestions never restrict sign-in: any domain can be entered and submitted with Enter.

The rail order is Profile, Home, Notifications, Bookmarks, Favorites, Search, with Writing anchored below. Saved-post tabs retain independent reading paths and use server Link cursors. Writing opens one native dialog overlay per session; its drafts persist locally across logout and app exit, isolated by instance and account. Posting requires write:statuses and an explicit Publish action. The composer shows audience, optional content warning, inline errors and a disabled in-flight state; retrying an unchanged draft reuses its idempotency key.

Login requests the full ordinary-account scopes read/write/follow/push, not administrative scopes. Interactions require the corresponding write scope; insufficient permissions explain that signing out and back in is required, with no supplementary authorization flow. A pending interaction disables that status across columns; confirmed fields update all loaded copies, and failures retain the prior state.

Post actions use immediate hover tint and a deeper pressed tint inside fixed rounded hit areas, without list movement or animation. A confirmed favourite uses a filled red heart (light #ce3150, dark #ff718b); inactive hearts remain outlines. Profile introductions group identity, readable paragraphs, optional metadata fields, and relationship counts separately. Metadata retains native selectable links and a neutral verification mark rather than introducing another accent palette.

Pending avatars, image thumbnails, feeds, login and publishing use MyGo Spinner in muted theme ink with accessible loading labels. Avatar and link-thumbnail slots keep fixed dimensions across loading, success and failure. Missing or failed avatars use static native Avatar fallbacks; completed or failed requests never keep spinning.

Writing uses the same MyGo DialogBase overlay as the image viewer and retains its draft when closed.

Other profiles show a native follow control opposite the avatar at the trailing edge of the profile header, with relationship hints below the button and full-width identity beneath the avatar row, backed by the authenticated instance’s relationship endpoint. Distinguish following, pending approval, followed-by and blocked states; never show a self-follow action. Fetch and write operations share per-account state across columns, disable duplicate requests, and update only after server confirmation. Authorization changes discard cached relationships; failed writes preserve the last confirmed state.

Reading polish: column outlines use text ink at 8% opacity in light mode and #333333 in dark mode and a 4% black shadow. Only a focused column in a multi-column path adds a 32% accent outline in light mode or #666666 in dark mode, and a 6 pt offset / 16 pt blur black shadow, at 16% opacity in light mode and 40% in dark mode; native keyboard focus remains enabled. High contrast retains the system border. Header separators use the shared quiet divider color.

Navigation, column titles, native menus and the composer use consistent Chinese labels. Post footer actions form a compact left group (reply, boost, favourite), with bookmark and external link aligned right. Hit areas remain at least 30 pt high and wide.

Post image slots reserve their height before downloading, using preview dimensions, then original dimensions, then a 16:9 fallback. Single images cap at 340 pt; multi-image thumbnails keep 146 pt height. Loaded images cover the same slot, including the fallback when dimensions are absent. Pending and failed images keep their slot; progress uses a single muted MyGo Slide indicator.

Application UI glyphs use bundled Lucide SVGs at their original 24-unit viewBox and 2-unit stroke, with the upstream license retained. Confirmed hearts and unread bells reuse the same paths with currentColor fill. External text links use Lucide arrow-up-right rather than a font glyph. Native framework controls and the Ceramic application logo retain their own artwork. The viewer uses official MyGo DialogBase for focus trapping and input isolation, fits images within the window, and uses a loaded thumbnail while the original is downloading.

Polls preserve API options, nullable unpublished counts, votes/voters, own selections and expiration. Posts, boosts, conversations and notification excerpts share the same poll renderer inside their content-warning boundary. Multiple-choice percentages use the number of voters when available. Unvoted active polls link to voting in the browser; there is no simulated in-app vote control.

Avatar rendering prefers `avatar`, retaining `avatar_static` for reduced motion and failed-load fallback. Bounded animated GIFs use official MyGo GIFDecoder/VideoPlayer with one player per visible avatar surface; hidden avatars release their player. Unsupported or oversized animations retain a still image. GIF animation is limited to 1 MiB compressed, 512 px per side, 120 frames and 16 million total canvas-frame pixels before full decoding. The compressed GIF is cached with other image data.

Custom emoji use only the emoji dictionary attached to their owning status, account or poll. Native RichText inline objects render the static image at 1.2× the text size, keeping links, paragraph wrapping and selection in the same text layout. Copying the paragraph restores the shortcode. Unknown, unsafe, loading or failed images keep their readable shortcode; standard Unicode emoji remain native text. This applies to post and notification bodies, display names, profiles, account metadata and poll choices. Custom emoji currently use static images rather than animation.


Explore defaults to post search, with accounts as the second segment. Browse sits below search in the empty Explore content area, with two inline, transparent Local and Federated buttons that open reusable columns in the Explore workspace; it is not part of the navigation rail. The profile rail avatar navigates on the first click; once selected, a native menu offers Settings and Log out. Settings persist the system/light/dark appearance using MyGo’s application theme source.

Writing uses a transparent-backdrop DialogBase popup with the bundled Ceramic logo beside its centered title, shared Lucide icons and Publish at the upper trailing edge, a quiet content-warning band, a borderless editor and a fixed bottom toolbar. The scrollable body keeps controls reachable at minimum size. Native file selection accepts up to four PNG/JPEG/GIF images (16 MiB and 24 million pixels each) with previews and descriptions. Uploads begin only on Publish; successful attachment IDs survive failed publishing retries. Polls have two to four distinct options, single/multiple choice, and preset duration. Images and polls are mutually exclusive. Drafts, including images and polls, persist locally across application restarts.

Post blank surfaces open the complete conversation. Account names, handles, avatars, links, content warnings, image previews and footer controls retain their own input handling; text selection never navigates. Profile, following, followers and conversation columns are unique by target within each workspace. Reopening reveals the existing column and preserves its reading position.

A publication job shares one cancelable context across image upload, asynchronous media processing and the final status request. Logout cancels pending work before discarding the composer; callbacks cannot restore the discarded session or draft. A request already accepted by the instance cannot be undone by local cancellation.

The composer popup fits within the main window with a 16 pt margin, at most 560 × 640 pt. Escape, Cmd-W and backdrop clicks dismiss it while retaining the draft; dismissal stays disabled during upload/publishing. A backdrop click never activates the underlying reader. Native image file selection is attached to the main window.

Publish uses the existing compact primary text button, without a paper-plane glyph. The adjacent draft entry opens a draft shelf in the same popup, with New, Resume and Delete controls. Closing, opening the shelf, starting a new draft, logout and normal application exit save the current draft atomically with private file permissions. The instance/account-scoped store includes attachments and retry identity; failures leave the editor open. Draft storage never schedules or publishes a status. Loaded shelf metadata releases image buffers until a draft is resumed.

Browse uses Lucide’s simple globe for Local and the classic CC0 five-node Fediverse community logo rendered in theme-matched monochrome for Federated. Both remain compact inline buttons without persistent backgrounds.

Application labels use the MyGo catalogs in locales, with Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Dutch. Settings uses a full-width native language select populated from the catalog languages and MyGo autonyms; App.SetLocale applies and persists the choice. Menus rebuild on locale changes. Protocol values and draft poll durations are independent of translated labels. This supersedes the earlier Chinese-only label rule.


Settings offers the native MyGo updater, with version, automatic checks and a manual check button. Update windows use the native plugin, and relaunch follows the normal draft-saving close path. Release manifests and archives are signed together.

Reply composition preserves the original visibility restriction and content warning, pre-fills participants except the current account, and saves any existing draft before opening a different reply. Reply identity persists with drafts and is submitted as in_reply_to_id. The reply target is visible above the editor.

Mention completion uses the token at the caret, with rune offsets and IME composition protection. Suggestions first use accounts already loaded, then a debounced, cancelable instance account search. Up/Down selects, Enter/Tab accepts, and Escape dismisses suggestions before the composer. Clicking a suggestion retains editor focus. Completion never submits the post.

Clickable account avatars grow to 105% over 140 ms on hover, with a fixed layout slot; pressing settles them and reduced motion disables scaling. Ordinary post text retains its existing appearance and conversation-opening behavior. Inline links share one interaction target across HTML spans, custom emoji and wrapped lines. Hover and keyboard focus use blue ink (light #1769c2, dark #79b8ff) without an underline; other text keeps its normal ink. Footer actions retain their local rounded hover and pressed tints.

Mention suggestions sit 6 pt below the editor. While suggestions are visible, the native text area fits its text from one to four lines, then scrolls; it does not reserve blank space beneath a short mention. Dismissing suggestions restores the normal composer editor height.

Own posts offer Edit and Delete in a native right-click context menu on the post surface and body, with no overflow button. The body menu retains native text-copy commands. Editing loads the server’s plain-text source into a compact DialogBase popup, changes the body and content warning, and preserves existing attachments, polls and audience. Delete requires an explicit confirmation. Requests disable duplicate actions; failures retain the editor, and confirmed changes update all loaded copies, including boosts and notifications. Older in-flight feed snapshots are retried after a confirmed mutation, and delayed streamed notifications cannot restore deleted posts. Session changes discard the popup, deleted-ID history and pending callbacks.

Circular avatar placeholders use the official MyGo loading.CircularDots effect. Other loading indicators use loading.Slide. Both use muted text color, retain accessible progress labels and follow the plugin’s reduced-motion behavior.

Keyboard hints use small neutral letter badges with 9-point semibold text, a quiet gray fill and a fine border over visible, enabled actions inside the focused column body, without changing layout. The navigation rail, column toolbars and other columns are excluded. Small-control badges sit on the trailing edge to keep icons readable; high-contrast mode retains strong borders and text. `f` enters hints, letters activate the existing action, Backspace corrects and Escape dismisses hints before the surrounding dialog. Text inputs and IME retain their keys. Hints belong only to the top dialog when open; scrolling, resizing or changed targets cancels the frozen mapping. Duplicate profile entry points share the author-name hint. `J/K` select the next/previous navigation item without wrapping; `H/L` remain column navigation. Native menus retain native keyboard interaction.

Navigation selection gives the glyph a 90 ms lift to 118% scale, then returns it to 100% over 220 ms while blending to the primary text color (near-black in light mode, light in dark mode). Selected Home, Notifications, Bookmarks, Favorites and the signed-out Profile glyph use filled silhouettes; Search retains its open lens for recognition. Hit targets and layout stay fixed. Account photos scale without tinting. Initial display and deselection stay still; reduced motion settles immediately.

Mention and hashtag links open reusable detail columns in the originating tab. Known mentions use instance-local account IDs; other marked mentions resolve through the logged-in instance. Hashtags use that instance’s timeline with normal refresh and pagination. Ordinary links continue to open in the browser.

The composer reads the instance’s character limit before enabling publication and shows used / allowed characters. Content warnings count toward the limit, emoji count by grapheme cluster, links use the server’s reserved length and remote mentions omit their domain. Over-limit drafts remain editable with clear feedback; unavailable limits offer Retry. Successful publication closes the composer after local draft cleanup. Failures preserve the draft and keep the composer open, including a warning when a published draft cannot be removed locally.

Notifications offer All and @ Mentions segments in the column toolbar. Each filter retains its own history, pagination and reading position; mentions are requested from the server by type. Both use the same session notification stream and global unread indicator.

Double-clicking Home, Favorites or Bookmarks in the navigation rail focuses that workspace’s root list and scrolls to its first post, retaining open detail columns. A single click preserves the workspace's focused column and reading position.

Boost sources use a quiet rounded label with an 18 pt avatar, source name and boost glyph, separate from the original author. The entire label opens the booster's profile. Private mentions use a quiet orange-to-transparent gradient on the post or notification surface, using the same treatment as replies and boosts with a plain envelope icon and label in the theme text color, without a pill background or outline; direct-mention notifications place it above the actor instead of repeating a generic mention label and audience label. Followers-only and public mentions retain their distinct visibility labels. Direct visibility takes precedence over reply, boost and content-warning tints; replies retain blue and boosts retain purple. High-contrast mode relies on explicit labels and system colors.

Profile introductions keep known identity and content visible during fetches. On first load, missing avatars use CircularDots in the circular avatar slot; missing biographies and unconfirmed counts use Slide placeholders. Loaded profiles remain visible during refresh, and confirmed empty biographies do not keep a placeholder.
